## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, authoritative, and deeply credible visual language for professional interpreter education and continuing accreditation. Inspired by elite postgraduate academies, collegiate continuing education divisions, and professional medical boards, the aesthetic emphasizes academic prestige, clarity, and structural rigor.

### Key Characteristics
- **Tone:** Authoritative, scholarly, dignified, and forward-looking.
- **Visual Stance:** Restrained and editorial. It rejects ephemeral SaaS gimmicks (no floating gradient blobs, playful pill badges, or cartoonish vector illustrations) in favor of crisp typographic hierarchy, asymmetric layouts, disciplined borders, and calibrated negative space.
- **Rhythm & Structure:** Alternating dense informational modules with airy, high-contrast editorial spreads, mimicking the cadence of high-end academic journals and formal institutional prospectuses.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for clinical legibility, high contrast, and academic sobriety, anchoring every view in deep maritime tones punctuated by an energetic yet disciplined teal.

- **Primary (`#103254`):** Oxford Deep Navy. Used for core brand identity, high-level typography, solid buttons, authoritative container backgrounds, and primary data marks.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#0F9FA5`):** Clinical Teal. Reserved for primary calls to action, active navigation states, interactive highlights, progress indicators, and verified credential tags.
- **Tertiary / Slate (`#536575`):** Slate Gray. Provides calibrated contrast for secondary copy, metadata, timestamps, course codes, and descriptive labels without dropping to low-contrast territory.
- **Neutral Light / Canvas (`#FFFFFF` & `#F4F7F9`):** Pure white serves as the primary canvas, while soft cool gray (`#F4F7F9`) defines distinct academic section bands, table headers, and structural module backgrounds.
- **Borders & Rules (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline cool gray dividers enforce architectural gridlines across panels, tables, and curriculum lists.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the structured geometric authority of **Manrope** for editorial headers with the high-legibility, objective rhythm of **Inter** for instructional content and technical documentation.

### Editorial Directives
- **Headings (Manrope):** Maintain tight, authoritative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) with consistent medium-to-bold weights. Never underline headings or apply ornamental gradients.
- **Body & Longform (Inter):** Generous line height (minimum 1.6×) supports comfortable scanning of detailed academic curricula, syllabus breakdowns, and regulatory standards.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Inter):** Small labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) for course taxonomy, RID/CEU credit codes, and status tags are styled with subtle uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em` to `0.06em`) to echo academic catalog indexing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 12-column architectural grid punctuated by asymmetrical division lines and editorial sidebars.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, with `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `3rem` outer margins. Hero sections use an asymmetrical 7:5 split (7 columns for programmatic title and syllabus narrative, 5 columns for structural overview, accreditation seals, and cohort status).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Content collapses into linear vertical streams with sticky secondary summaries.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Horizontal rules replace multi-column dividers.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Anti-Template Rhythm:** Replace homogeneous 3-column card layouts with alternating row-based curriculum schedules, two-tier catalog indices, and side-by-side comparative dossiers.
- **Whitespace Discipline:** Section transitions require decisive spacing (`space-xl` scaled to 4rem–6rem on desktop) separated by subtle `#E2E8F0` hairline rules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through planar layering, surface tonality, and hairline borders rather than heavy ambient drop shadows or floating elevations.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` canvas. 
- **Level 1 (Subdued Containers & Alternates):** Solid `#F4F7F9` background with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Shadows are prohibited.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0`. On user hover, the border shifts decisively to `rgba(16, 50, 84, 0.25)` accompanied by a minimal, highly disciplined directional offset shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(16, 50, 84, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Contextual Menus):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and an authoritative ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 50, 84, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a restrained **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`4px` / `0.25rem` baseline radius). This preserves an architectural, printed-document geometry suited for an accredited institution.

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius. Rounded pill shapes are prohibited to avoid a casual or tech-startup aesthetic.
- **Structural Modules (Cards, Modals, Callout Panels):** `6px` to `8px` (`0.375rem` to `0.5rem`) maximum radius.
- **Dividers & Outlines:** Crisp, 1px vector lines with zero blur or soft feathering.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#103254` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Flat finish, `4px` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding, `label-lg` weight. Hover: `#0F9FA5` background with immediate transition. Focus: `2px` offset outline in `#0F9FA5`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Solid `#0F9FA5` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for conversion inflection points (e.g., "Enroll Now", "Apply for Fellowship").
- **Outline / Academic:** Transparent background with `1px solid #103254` and `#103254` text. Hover: `#F4F7F9` background.
- **Ghost:** Text-only in `#103254` with subtle right-chevron icon (`16px`). Hover: Text color shifts to `#0F9FA5` with `2px` forward icon translation.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#103254`. Focus state: border `1px solid #103254` with a `2px` concentric ring in `rgba(15, 159, 165, 0.2)`. Labels sit strictly outside above the field in `label-md` `#536575`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px` geometric controls. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px solid #536575`. Selected: `#103254` fill with white checkmark or center pip. Focus ring in `#0F9FA5`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Accreditation / CEU Badges:** Background `#F4F7F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#103254`, `label-md` styling, uppercase tracking. Never styled as playful colorful bubbles.
- **Active / Verified Tag:** Background `rgba(15, 159, 165, 0.08)`, border `1px solid #0F9FA5`, text `#0F9FA5`, prefixed by a clean square status indicator.

### Cards & Syllabus Modules
- **Academic Program Rows:** Rather than vertical cards, present courses in structured horizontal row registers. Left: Course code and credential tier. Middle: Title, learning outcomes, and instructor credentials. Right: Date, CEU units, and enrollment action.
- **Overview Panels:** `#FFFFFF` or `#F4F7F9` surfaces enclosed by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Padding uses `space-lg`. Titles must be set in `headline-md` `#103254` followed by `#536575` body copy.

### Institutional Proof & Metrics
- Numbers are displayed in `headline-2xl` `#103254` paired with an immediate `#0F9FA5` baseline metric indicator. Supporting descriptor sits beneath in `body-sm` `#536575`. Avoid decorative circular icons around statistics.
